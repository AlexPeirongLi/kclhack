Welcome to Vector Web

The web, but you walk it. Every topic is an island and every signboard is a live website. Etch a question into the back of your compass and the needle points to where its answer lives.

Click once, then move the mouse to look   Esc frees the mouse
WASD move   Shift sprint   Space jump, and again against a wall to wall-jump
C crouch, or slide if you are sprinting
E turn the compass over and type   Enter seek
F use the signboard in front of you   G ask the guide about it
